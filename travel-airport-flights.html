Stage 04 · Reading & PA Announcements

A Tight Connection in Frankfurt

Nora is flying from Madrid to Oslo with a 75-minute layover in Frankfurt. Her luggage is checked through to her final destination, so she only has a small carry-on backpack.

Because of strong headwinds, her first flight lands 30 minutes late. As soon as she enters the terminal, she checks the departure screens. Her connecting flight is still boarding, but the gate has changed from A18 to B42, and she has to pass passport control on the way.

Instead of panicking, Nora shows her boarding pass to a ground staff member, who points her toward the fast-track transfer lane. She reaches Gate B42 just as final boarding is called—tired, relieved, and glad she packed essentials in her hand luggage.

Stage 05 · Multilevel Upgrade Lab

From Simple Requests to Natural Polite English

A1–A2 → B1–B2 Upgrade
1. ASKING FOR HELP / SEATS

A1–A2: Can I have a window seat, please?

B1–B2: Would it be possible to switch to a window seat if there are any left?

2. EXPLAINING A PROBLEM

A1–A2: My bag is missing. It didn't arrive.

B1–B2: I checked a suitcase in Madrid, but it hasn't come out on the baggage carousel.

3. ASKING ABOUT CONNECTIONS

A1–A2: Where is Gate B42? Is my flight late?

B1–B2: Could you tell me the quickest way to Gate B42? I have a tight connection.
