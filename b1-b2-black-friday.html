    Stage 04 · Reading & B1/B2 Structures

    Behind the "70% Off" Banner

    Consumer Insights · 180 Words B1/B2 Reading

    Every November, millions of shoppers stay up past midnight hoping to snap up a bargain before stock runs out. Yet consumer watchdogs warn that not every red price tag is the steal it appears to be. In many cases, retailers quietly raise prices in October so that a Black Friday discount looks far more generous than it actually is.

    Psychologists also point to scarcity tactics and countdown timers—often called dark patterns—that trigger fear of missing out. If shoppers had taken ten minutes to compare price histories, they would have realized that several "doorbuster" deals were cheaper three months earlier. Still, when planned carefully with a strict budget, seasonal sales can be a smart way to replace essentials without breaking the bank.
