Article · Short-Shift Trials

Why Some Teams Test Four-Day Weeks

Several medium-sized companies recently ran six-month trials cutting working time to four days with no drop in pay. Rather than working longer hours on the remaining days, staff were asked to cut unproductive meetings and protect quiet focus blocks.

At first, managers worried client response times might slow down. By the end of the trial, however, most teams met their targets on schedule, while employees reported lower stress and fewer sick days—though a few roles that require constant customer coverage still needed rotating shifts.
