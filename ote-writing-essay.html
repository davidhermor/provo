Stage 05 · Annotated Exam Model (146 Words)

X-Ray of a High-Scoring OTE Essay

EXAM TASK

In your English class, you have been talking about technology in education. Now your teacher has asked you to write an essay.

“Should schools replace printed textbooks with digital tablets?”

Highlight Layers:
Linkers & Contrast
B2 Lexicon
Complex B2 Grammar (Inversion, Passive, Non-defining Relative Clause, Third Conditional)
MODEL ESSAY · 4 PARAGRAPHS WORD COUNT: 152 WORDS ✓

Nowadays, it is widely debated whether traditional textbooks should be replaced by tablets. In my opinion, although digital devices offer clear benefits, printed books remain essential.

Firstly, not only do tablets reduce heavy backpacks, but updating digital materials is also cost-effective. However, constant notifications often undermine concentration, which negatively affects exam performance.

Furthermore, if schools had considered the environmental footprint earlier, fewer resources would have been wasted on short-lived hardware generating electronic waste.

To sum up, despite their convenience, tablets should complement printed textbooks rather than replace them entirely.
