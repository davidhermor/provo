Stage 08 · Short Writing Task

Short Mood & Routine Note (Under 80–100 Words)

Keep it under 80–100 words
Writing Prompt

Write a short message or diary note (around 50–80 words, and never over 100 words) about how you feel today.

  • • Say how you are feeling right now or today.
  • • Include 2–3 feeling adjectives (e.g. tired, excited, calm, stressed).
  • • Use very, really, or so once or twice.
  • • Say what helps you feel better when your mood drops.
