    Stage 04

    Grammar Guide & Check

    Visual diagrams, clear rules for Present Simple, live form inspector, and summary check.

    REPEATED PATTERN Habits · Facts · Timetables · Stative

    Present Simple anchors regular habits, general truths, fixed schedules, and stative verbs (know, prefer, want, need, love, belong).

    Key Spelling Rule

    He / She / It → -s / -es / -ies

    In negatives and questions with doesn't / does, return to the base verb.

    01 · Spelling · He / She / It

    Third-Person Endings

    • General verbs: +s (works, plays, reads).
    • Ending in -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: +es (watches, goes).
    • Consonant + y: -ies (study → studies).
    • Irregular: have → has.
    02 · Do / Does

    Negatives & Questions

    • I / you / we / they: don't + base verb.
    • He / she / it: doesn't + base verb.
    • Questions: Do / Does + subject + base verb?
    • Never add -s to the main verb after does or doesn't.
    03 · Adverbs & States

    Position & Stative Verbs

    • Before main verbs: She always drinks tea.
    • After verb to be: He is always on time.
    • Stative verbs (know, prefer, want, belong) describe states rather than actions in progress.
