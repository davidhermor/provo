Are You a Window Shopper or a Bargain Hunter?

Use the style & price adjectives from Stage 04 in your answers!

Key Expression · Window Shopping

Window shopping — looking at clothes and products in store windows for fun without buying anything.

1. Do you enjoy window shopping, or do you only go into stores when you need something?
2. What is the best bargain you have ever found in the sales?
3. Do you prefer buying brand-new clothes or second-hand / vintage items?
4. Have you ever bought something online that didn't fit when it arrived?
5. What is more important when you buy shoes: comfort, price, or style?

Stage 08 · Final Speaking Task

My Favorite Outfit or Purchase

Tell your classmates about an item of clothing or outfit you love wearing.

Use These 6 Points to Help You:

  • 1. What is the item or outfit? (jacket, trainers, dress, etc.)
  • 2. Where and when did you buy it? (online, mall, market, in the sales)
  • 3. What color, size, and material is it? (cotton, denim, leather, etc.)
  • 4. Was it expensive or a good bargain?
  • 5. When do you usually wear it? (every day, weekends, special events)
  • 6. Why is it so comfortable or special to you?
