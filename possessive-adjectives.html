    POSSESSIVE + NOUN Always followed by a noun
    Golden Rule

    Match the owner, not the noun

    A man's car = his car · A woman's car = her car.

    01 · Full Table

    All 7 Forms

    • I → my · you → your
    • he → his · she → her · it → its
    • we → our · they → their
    02 · Contraction Traps

    No Apostrophe for Possession

    • its (possessive) vs. it's (it is / it has).
    • your (possessive) vs. you're (you are).
    • their (possessive) vs. they're (they are) / there (place).
    03 · Adjectives vs. Pronouns

    Before Noun vs. Alone

    • Possessive adjective + noun: This is my bag.
    • Possessive pronoun (standalone): This bag is mine. (hers, ours, theirs).
