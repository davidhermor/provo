    Stage 04

    Grammar Contrast

    Clear rules and live tense-flipping to compare routines, facts, and actions in progress.

    PRESENT SIMPLE Habits · Facts · Stative

    Base verb (+s / +es / +ies for he, she, it) for repeated routines, permanent facts, timetables, and stative verbs (know, prefer, want, belong, believe).

    Habit: She drinks coffee every morning.
    Fact: Water boils at 100 °C.
    Stative: I prefer quiet mornings.
    PRESENT CONTINUOUS Right Now · Temporary

    am / is / are + verb-ing for actions happening at the moment of speaking, temporary projects/trends around now, or arranged plans.

    Right now: She is recording a voice note.
    Temporary: We are testing a new routine this week.
    Dynamic shift: I'm thinking about our trip today.
