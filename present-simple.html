    Stage 04

    Grammar Guide

    When we use Present Simple, how to form it, and where frequency adverbs go.

    Core Timeline

    Past ← ● ——— ● ——— ● ——— ● → Future

    Repeated habits, permanent truths, and fixed timetables.

    4 Main Uses of Present Simple

    1. Habits & Routines

    I walk every morning.

    2. Permanent Facts

    Water boils at 100 °C. / She lives in Spain.

    3. Fixed Timetables

    The train leaves at 8:15 AM.

    4. Stative Verbs

    We prefer tea. / He knows the answer.

    Spelling · He / She / It

    Third-Person Endings

    • General: +s (works, plays, reads)
    • -ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: +es (watches, goes)
    • Consonant + y: -ies (study → studies)
    • Irregular: have → has
    Do / Does

    Negatives & Questions

    • I / You / We / They: don't + base
    • He / She / It: doesn't + base
    • Questions: Do / Does + subject + base?
    • Golden rule: never add -s after does/doesn't.
    Word Order

    Frequency Adverbs

    • Before main verbs: She always drinks tea.
    • After verb to be: He is always punctual.
    • Scale: always → usually → often → sometimes → hardly ever → never.
