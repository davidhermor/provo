Stage 04 · Short Reading & Chat

Everyone Looks Like They're Having More Fun

Scroll for ten minutes at 11:30 p.m. and it can feel like everyone else is at a rooftop party, hitting PRs at the gym, or traveling somewhere sunny—while you are just lying on your bed eating cereal. Most teens know logically that feeds are curated, yet doomscrolling late at night still messes with your mood.

A lot of what gets posted is a highlight reel: the best angle, the best lighting, or a clip filmed on the one sunny afternoon out of a rainy week. Mutings, taking a break from main-character energy when you just want to chill, or keeping a finsta / alt account for real friends are ways teens actually protect their space without deleting everything.
