Are You a Foodie?

Use the new taste & texture adjectives from Stage 04 in your answers!

Key Word · Foodie

Foodie — is a person who really enjoys food, likes to taste new dishes, and often talks about their favorite meals and places to eat.

1. Are you a foodie?
2. What is your all-time favorite food?
3. Have you ever had an excellent or terrible restaurant experience? Describe it.
4. Do you like spicy food, or do you prefer mild flavors?
5. What makes a restaurant great for you: the food, the price, or the service?

Stage 08 · Final Speaking Task

Famous Dish Presentation

Tell your classmates about a popular dish from your country.

Use These 6 Points to Help You:

  • 1. What is the name of the dish?
  • 2. What ingredients are in it? (meat, vegetables, rice, etc.)
  • 3. How do people cook it? (fried, boiled, grilled, baked, etc.)
  • 4. When do people eat it? (every day, special occasions, breakfast/lunch/dinner)
  • 5. How does it taste? (sweet, salty, spicy, mild, etc.)
  • 6. Why do you like it or recommend it?
