    Core Contrast

    Stability & Habits vs. Live Action & Change

    Choose the tense by meaning and time context, not by single-word guessing alone.

    Present Simple · Form & Use

    Routines, Facts & Timetables

    • + I/we/you/they work · he/she/it works / watches / studies.
    • − don't / doesn't + base verb (no -s after doesn't).
    • ? Do / Does + subject + base verb?
    • Used for habits, general truths, and schedules.
    Present Continuous · Form & Use

    In Progress & Temporary

    • + / − / ? am / is / are (+ not) + verb-ing.
    • Spelling: drop silent -e (make → making); double CVC consonant when stressed (run → running).
    • Used for actions right now, temporary periods around now, and changing trends.
    Stative vs. Dynamic Shift

    Verbs That Change Meaning

    • Stative (Simple): I think she's right (opinion); We have two cats (possession).
    • Dynamic (Continuous): I'm thinking about the trip (active thought); We're having dinner (experiencing).
