    REPEATED HABITS & FACTS base verb / verb + -s (-es / -ies)

    Present Simple expresses regular habits, routines, permanent facts, and schedules—not just what is happening in this second.

    He / She / It Rule

    Add -s in affirmatives, use DOES / DOESN'T

    Say “She works” and “She doesn't work” (no extra -s after doesn't).

    01 · He / She / It Endings

    Third-Person Spelling

    • Most verbs: +s (work → works, live → lives).
    • -s, -sh, -ch, -x, -o: +es (watch → watches, go → goes).
    • Consonant + y: -ies (study → studies); vowel + y: +s (play → plays).
    • Irregular: have → has.
    02 · Do & Does

    Negatives & Questions

    • I / you / we / they: don't + base verb; Do you...?
    • He / she / it: doesn't + base verb; Does she...?
    • Never add -s to the main verb after doesn't or does.
    03 · Adverb Position

    Frequency Adverbs

    • Before main verbs: I usually walk to work.
    • After to be: He is always on time.
    • Never already has a negative meaning: I never drink coffee at night.
