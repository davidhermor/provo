Stage 03 · Grammar Architecture (1/2)

How & Why We Use the Passive

Core Formula: BE + Past Participle (V3)
VISUAL MECHANICS · ACTIVE → PASSIVE SHIFT
1. UNKNOWN DOER

We don't know who did it

“My bike was stolen outside the station.” (Adding by someone is redundant.)

2. OBVIOUS DOER

Context makes it obvious

“The suspect was arrested at dawn.” (Clearly by the police.)

3. PROCESSES & FACTS

Focus on the process/object

“Coffee beans are roasted at 200 °C.” (Common in science, essays, and reports.)

4. HIGHLIGHTING THE WORK

Keep the topic as subject

“Guernica was painted by Picasso in 1937.” (Use by only when the agent adds vital info.)
