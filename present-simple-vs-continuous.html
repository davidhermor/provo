    PRESENT SIMPLE Habits, Facts & Routines

    Use the base verb (+s / +es for he, she, it) for repeated actions, permanent truths, schedules, and stative verbs.

    + She drinks mate every morning.
    − They don't work on Sundays.
    ? Does he speak English?
    PRESENT CONTINUOUS Right Now & Temporary

    Use am / is / are + verb-ing for actions happening at the moment of speaking, temporary situations, or fixed arrangements.

    + She is recording a voice note right now.
    − They aren't watching TV this week.
    ? Are you meeting Tom at 6?

    Dynamic / Action Verbs (Both Tenses)

    Physical or mental actions that can be repeated or paused: run, cook, walk, study, travel, work.

    Meaning shift: I think about grammar (opinion) vs. I'm thinking about our trip (mental process right now).

    Stative Verbs (Usually Present Simple)

    States, emotions, possession, and senses: know, believe, want, need, love, hate, prefer, own, belong, seem.

    Avoid: I am knowing / She is wanting → Use I know / She wants.
