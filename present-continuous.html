    Stage 04

    Grammar Guide & Check

    Visual timeline, clear rules for Present Continuous, live form inspector, and summary check.

    ACTION IN PROGRESS am / is / are + verb-ing

    Present Continuous describes actions happening at the moment of speaking, temporary projects around now, and arranged near-future plans.

    Essential Structure

    Always keep BE (am / is / are)

    Say “She is working”, never *“She working”.

    01 · Spelling -ing

    How to Add -ing

    • Standard verbs: +ing (play → playing, read → reading).
    • Silent -e: drop -e + ing (make → making, write → writing).
    • Short stressed CVC: double consonant (sit → sitting, run → running).
    • -ie verbs: lie → lying.
    02 · Forms & Contractions

    Positive, Negative & Questions

    • + I'm / She's / They're + verb-ing.
    • − I'm not / He isn't / We aren't + verb-ing.
    • ? Am I / Is she / Are you + verb-ing?
    • Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, she isn't.
    03 · 3 Main Uses

    When to Use It

    • Right now: Look! It's raining.
    • Temporary: I'm reading a great book this week.
    • Arranged plans: We're meeting Tom at 6 PM.
